# Radiant Women's Circle — Spirituality & Sexuality

**A two-part programme. Two live 2-hour sessions on Zoom.**
**In the voice of the "Become a Sexually Empowered Radiant Woman" course**

> Discover sacred practices to enhance intimacy and deepen connection during lovemaking.

For most of us, sex and the sacred were kept in separate rooms. One was holy, the other
was shameful, hidden, or purely physical. This programme puts them back together. Because
your sexuality isn't the opposite of your spirituality. It's one of its deepest doorways.

- **Session 1 — The Sacred Woman.** Come home to your body and your desire as sacred. Shed
  the shame, and learn presence and breath as the ground of everything.
- **Session 2 — The Sacred Union.** Bring that presence into lovemaking: slowness, eye
  gaze, breath together, conscious touch, giving and receiving with an open heart.

**Safety is the ground, and here it's sacred.** Nothing in this work is ever pushed. You go
at your own pace, you can always pass, and consent, with yourself and with a partner, is
the beginning of anything sacred. The practices we do together are breath, presence and
reflection. The intimate practices are yours to take home, for yourself or with a partner
you choose.

---

## Running it on Zoom

- **Breakouts in pairs** for sharing and reflection (the intimate body practices are
  take-home, not on camera). Assign Partner A / B by first-name alphabetical order.
- **Broadcast the prompt** to all rooms before opening; re-broadcast the swap at halfway.
- **Camera optional** for the guided practices; invite women to close their eyes and drop in.
- **The listener witnesses, doesn't fix.** Say it before each breakout.
- **Re-read the sacred-space agreement** at the top of both sessions: what's shared here
  stays here; we witness; you can always pass or slow down; there's no such thing as too
  much here; this is a judgement-free, reverent space.

# SESSION 1 — The Sacred Woman (120 min)

**Intention:** she reconnects to her body and her desire as sacred, begins to release
shame, and learns breath and presence as the foundation of sacred sexuality.

| Time | Part | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | **Arrive & soften** | Land in the body |
| 0:12–0:30 | **Sacred, not shameful** | What sacred sexuality is; safety as sacred ground |
| 0:30–0:45 | **Practice: Sacred breath** | Guided breath and grounding into the body |
| 0:45–1:03 | **Breakout 1** | The story I was told about my body and desire |
| 1:03–1:10 | **Break** | |
| 1:10–1:28 | **Your body is a temple** | Body appreciation, reclaiming pleasure |
| 1:28–1:45 | **Breakout 2** | From shame to sacred |
| 1:45–2:00 | **Take-home ritual & close** | A practice to carry |

## Part 1 — Arrive & soften (0:00–0:12)

Welcome, candle, sacred-space agreements.

> Eyes closed. Feet on the floor. Soften your jaw, your shoulders, your belly. Three slow
> breaths, all the way down into your pelvis. Tonight we're doing something most of us were
> never allowed to do: we're treating your body, your pleasure and your desire as holy.

One-word go-round: "How are you arriving?"

## Part 2 — Sacred, not shameful (0:12–0:30)

> Somewhere along the way we were taught that sex was dirty, dangerous, or something we do
> for someone else. And that spirituality was up here, in the mind, in the light, far away
> from the body. But the old traditions knew something we forgot. That the body is a
> temple. That pleasure is a prayer. That your sexual energy is your life force, the same
> energy that creates life, creates art, creates you.
>
> Sacred sexuality isn't a technique. It's a turning toward. Turning toward your body with
> reverence instead of judgement. Turning toward pleasure as something holy, not something
> to earn or be ashamed of. Turning toward another, when you choose to, with your whole
> presence.

**Safety as sacred ground:**
> And none of it is sacred without safety. Sacred means you're never pushed, never
> performing, never abandoning yourself. It means your yes is real because your no is
> allowed. Consent, first with yourself, is the beginning of anything holy. So everything
> tonight is an invitation. You take what's yours and leave the rest.

**Reflection questions** (pose, then into the practice and breakout):
- What was I taught about my body and my sexuality, and who taught me?
- Where do I still carry shame, and whose voice is it in?
- What would it feel like to treat my body as sacred?

## Part 3 — Practice: Sacred breath & grounding (0:30–0:45)

Guide this slowly. Cameras optional, eyes closed.

> Both feet on the floor. One hand on your heart, one hand low on your belly. Breathe down,
> long and slow, all the way into your pelvis, into your womb space. This is the seat of
> your life force.
>
> With each breath in, imagine drawing warmth up through your body. With each breath out,
> let a little more shame, a little more armouring, soften and melt.
>
> Silently, say to your body: "I'm here. I love you. Thank you." Notice what softens.
> Notice what wakes up. Nothing to make happen. Just breath, and presence, and coming home.

Sit in it for several minutes. This is the foundational sacred practice, everything builds
on breath and presence.

## Part 4 — Breakout 1: The story I was told (0:45–1:03)

**Broadcast to the rooms:**
> In pairs. Partner A, 6 minutes:
> - "The story I was told about my body and my sexuality was..."
> - "The shame I still carry is... and I think it's in the voice of..."
> - "What I'd love to believe instead is..."
> Partner B, just witness. No fixing. Then swap.

**Main-room go-round (3 min):** one sentence each, "What I'd love to believe about my body
is..."

## Break (1:03–1:10)

## Part 5 — Your body is a temple (1:10–1:28)

Teach and guide a short body-appreciation practice.

> Your body deserves your love and attention. I think of mine like a beloved. If I condemn
> her, she shuts down. If I love her, care for her, she opens all her miracles to me.
>
> Let's honour her now. Bring your hands to your own heart, then your belly, then wherever
> feels right. As you touch each part, silently thank her. What do your hands do for you?
> Your hips? Your belly, that can hold and grow life? Send each part gratitude. Send it
> love.
>
> This is the foundation of reclaiming your pleasure: a body that feels adored instead of
> judged. Pleasure can't open in a body that's bracing for criticism. It opens in a body
> that feels safe and cherished.

## Part 6 — Breakout 2: From shame to sacred (1:28–1:45)

**Reflection question first:** "What pleasure did I stop letting myself feel, and what
would it mean to give it back to myself?"

**Broadcast to the rooms:**
> In pairs. Partner A, 6 minutes:
> - "A pleasure I stopped letting myself feel is..."
> - "One small, sacred pleasure I could give myself this week is..."
> - "The part of my body I'm ready to make peace with is..."
> Partner B, witness with warmth. Then swap.

**Main-room (2 min):** "Name the one sacred pleasure you're claiming this week."

## Part 7 — Take-home ritual & close (1:45–2:00)

**Take-home (for yourself, or with a partner you choose):**
> Each day this week, ten minutes. Light a candle. Hand on your heart, hand on your belly.
> Breathe into your body and say, "I'm here. I love you. Thank you." Then do one small thing
> that treats your body as sacred: a slow bath, silk on your skin, moving to music,
> dressing for no one but yourself. You're teaching your body that pleasure is safe and
> holy again.

Closing round, one line each: "The way I'll honour my body this week is..." Circle answers:
"She is sacred." Blow out the candle together.

# SESSION 2 — The Sacred Union (120 min)

**Intention:** she learns sacred practices to bring presence, slowness and reverence into
lovemaking, and to give and receive with an open heart, from the safety and self-love
built in Session 1.

| Time | Part | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | **Arrive, soften, recap** | Re-ground in the sacred |
| 0:12–0:30 | **Slow is sacred** | Presence over performance; lovemaking as meditation |
| 0:30–0:45 | **Practice: Eye gaze & breath** | Guided presence practice |
| 0:45–1:03 | **Breakout 1** | What helps me feel safe and connected |
| 1:03–1:10 | **Break** | |
| 1:10–1:28 | **Sacred space & conscious touch** | Creating ritual; the dance of love |
| 1:28–1:45 | **Breakout 2** | Speaking desire, receiving like a queen |
| 1:45–2:00 | **Couple's ritual & close** | A practice to carry home |

## Part 1 — Arrive, soften, recap (0:00–0:12)

Soften as before. Recap the ground:

> Last time we made your body sacred again. Tonight we bring that reverence to connection.
> Remember: safety first, always. Sacred union begins with your own yes.

Go-round: "One way I honoured my body this week was..."

**Reflection questions** (pose now for later):
- When do I feel truly safe and connected with another? What creates it?
- What do I long for in intimacy that I've never said out loud?
- What helps me stay present instead of performing or disappearing?

## Part 2 — Slow is sacred (0:12–0:30)

> Slow is the secret. Most lovemaking rushes straight to stimulation and the goal. But you
> can't build deep connection at full speed. Sacred union isn't about performance or
> arriving anywhere. It's presence. It's making love the way you'd meditate or pray: fully
> here, fully awake, in no hurry at all.
>
> When you slow right down, something opens. There's space for feeling. Space for
> vulnerability. And vulnerability is what lets two people actually meet, instead of two
> bodies going through the motions. Slowness turns sex into communion.

## Part 3 — Practice: Eye gaze & synchronised breath (0:30–0:45)

Guide this. On Zoom, women can do it solo (with a hand on the heart, or gazing softly at
their own eyes in a small mirror or the camera) as a rehearsal for home.

> Sit tall. Soften your gaze. Breathe slowly, in and out. Imagine sitting knee to knee with
> your beloved. You breathe together. You simply witness and are witnessed. No talking, no
> fixing, no performing.
>
> Notice the urge to look away, to laugh, to break it. Stay anyway. Underneath the
> awkwardness there's a person who can be truly seen. Tonight, practise letting yourself be
> seen, starting with yourself.

## Part 4 — Breakout 1: Safe and connected (0:45–1:03)

**Broadcast to the rooms:**
> In pairs. Partner A, 6 minutes:
> - "I feel most safe and connected with someone when..."
> - "What pulls me out of presence in intimacy is..."
> - "What helps me come back is..."
> Partner B, witness. Then swap.

**Main-room (3 min):** "One thing that helps you feel safe and connected is..."

## Break (1:03–1:10)

## Part 5 — Sacred space & conscious touch (1:10–1:28)

> You can make lovemaking a ritual, and it starts before you ever touch. Create a special
> space. Candles. Soft music. Fabrics that feel beautiful, silk, fur, warmth. A door that
> closes. You're telling your nervous system, and your beloved: this is sacred time.
>
> Then, the dance of love, and it's slow. Breathe together first. Eye gaze. See each
> other's beauty, power and vulnerability. Then touch, slowly. Feather the whole body,
> fingertips sending love, no destination. Take turns giving and receiving. Slowness makes
> space for feeling, and feeling is what makes it sacred.

Frame it as presence, not technique: the point isn't what you do, it's how present you are
while you do it.

## Part 6 — Breakout 2: Speak your desire, receive like a queen (1:28–1:45)

**Reflection question first:** "What do I desire that I've been too afraid to ask for, and
what would it feel like to simply receive?"

**Broadcast to the rooms:**
> In pairs. Partner A, 6 minutes, practising two things out loud:
> - Speak a desire, cleanly and warmly: "Something I long for in intimacy is..." (not a
>   complaint, a desire).
> - Practise receiving: let Partner B offer you a genuine appreciation, and simply say
>   "thank you," let it land, notice the urge to deflect.
> Partner B, witness, and offer that appreciation. Then swap.

**Main-room (2 min):** "What was harder, speaking your desire, or receiving?"

## Part 7 — Couple's ritual & close (1:45–2:00)

**Take-home (for yourself, or with a partner you choose):**
> This week, create one pocket of sacred time. Light a candle. Sit knee to knee, or hand on
> heart if you're solo. Breathe together for a few minutes. Eye gaze for three. Then, slow,
> reverent touch or self-touch, with no goal at all, just presence and love. Notice how
> different it feels when you're fully here.

Closing round, one line each: "The reverence I'm bringing to intimacy is..." Circle
answers: "Sacred." Blow out the candle together.

## Facilitator notes (both sessions)

- **Reverence, not performance.** Keep the tone warm, unhurried, and shame-free. This is
  sacred, not clinical and not crude.
- **Safety and consent are the frame.** Everything is an invitation; nothing is pushed.
  Repeat that women can pass, slow down, or keep anything private.
- **On Zoom, the intimate practices are take-home.** In the room we do breath, presence and
  reflection. The touch and lovemaking practices are theirs to do at home, solo or with a
  chosen partner.
- **Protect the witness role** in every breakout: listen, don't fix.
- **Hold the container.** Emotion, tears, or memory may surface around body shame. Let the
  room hold it; keep it safe; suggest one-to-one support if something big arises.
- **Inclusive language.** Not everyone has a partner; frame every practice so it works
  beautifully solo, as self-love and self-communion.
