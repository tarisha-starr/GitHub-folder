The course

## Three months. Twelve weekly sessions. A small group of women

SERW is the original work. Where most of what The Love Adventure teaches first took shape. Three months of group work for women who want to come home to their sexual self, before, after, or independent of any relationship.

Topics across the twelve weeks include: shame and the inheritance, body and sensation, voice and consent, desire (yours, not his), the erotic identity, polarity work, the inner critic, the inner woman, ritual and practice, rage and power, and the integration weeks at the end.

SERW is the door to the membership. Most members start here.
