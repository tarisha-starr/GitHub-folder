About the pilgrimage

The Bliss Pilgrimage is a four-day women-only retreat at Lake Tarawera, Rotorua. The lake itself is medicine. The pilgrimage is a yearly commitment to slowing down, going inwards, and remembering pleasure as a direct route to wisdom.
