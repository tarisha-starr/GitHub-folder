Why this exists

“I love him. I just don’t want him.”

The most common thing women over 40 say to me, in almost the same words. Love is fine. Sex feels like a chore. You’re more flatmates than lovers. You don’t want to leave him. You want to find him again.

This is the membership for that. Not a course on how to be sexier for your partner. A monthly circle for the woman who’s ready to come back to herself, so she can come back to her relationship.

This is for you if:

  • You’re over 40 and in a long relationship you don’t want to leave
  • You love him but the spark has gone flat
  • Sex has become one more thing on the list
  • You want to feel like a woman again, not just a manager
  • You want to communicate your needs without it becoming a fight
  • You’re ready to do the work, even if your partner isn’t

What you get every month:

  • Two live group sessions with Tarisha (1.5–2 hours each)
  • A monthly theme: intimacy, desire, communication, body, polarity, voice
  • A private community of women doing the same work
  • Practices and homework you can use immediately
  • Recordings of every session, available to you for life of membership
  • (Premium tier) Invitation to book private therapy time with Tarisha
