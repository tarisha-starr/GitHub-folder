What it is

## When you want to bring just yourself

Sometimes the relationship work has to start solo. You can’t drag a partner into therapy. You can change yourself, the field shifts, and they often follow. This is the room for that work.

Tarisha works with women on relationship dynamics, sexual self, attachment patterns, the narratives carried from parents and previous relationships, and the body’s part in all of it. EFT (Levels 1 and 2), Hakomi somatic psychotherapy, biodynamic breathwork facilitation, somatic trauma work, and sex therapy. Tarisha holds a Master’s in Counselling and is a member of the New Zealand Association of Counsellors (NZAC).

Sessions are 75 minutes, in our Auckland practice or via Zoom. Most clients come weekly or fortnightly. We’ll talk on a discovery call first to see if the fit is right.
