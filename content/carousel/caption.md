## Facebook caption (your full post)

If one of you wants sex and the other one keeps finding reasons not to...

You're not broken. Your marriage isn't broken.

You're stuck in a cycle. The cycle is the problem, not either of you.

I'm a relationship and sex therapist, trained in Emotionally Focused Therapy, and I sit with couples who love each other and haven't touched each other in months. And so many of them are running the same dance.

THE EMOTIONAL CYCLE

One partner reaches. They push, they criticise, they follow you down the hallway wanting to talk about it. Underneath? They're scared. "Do I matter to you? Are you still there?"

The other partner pulls back. They go quiet, go to the shed, go to their phone. Underneath? They're scared too. "I keep getting it wrong. I'm failing you. If I stay still maybe it'll pass."

So the reacher reaches harder. And the withdrawer withdraws further. Round and round.

Neither of you is the bad guy. You're both trying to protect the connection, in opposite ways.

THE SEXUAL CYCLE

Take that exact same dance into the bedroom.

The partner who wants more sex initiates. A hand on the hip. A look. A "so... tonight?"

The other partner feels it like pressure. Their body tightens. Their brakes slam on. They say "I'm tired", and sometimes they are tired, but mostly they're tired of feeling like they owe something.

So they start avoiding. Going to bed early. Going to bed late. No more long hugs in the kitchen, because a hug might turn into a question.

And the initiating partner feels rejected. Again. And again. So they either push harder... or they go cold and stop trying.

Both of them end up lonely. Both of them think the other one doesn't care.

These two cycles feed each other. When you feel disconnected during the day, your body doesn't want to open at night. That's not a libido problem. That's a safety and emotional connection problem. And when sex keeps going wrong at night, you feel more disconnected during the day.

That's why "just schedule date night" doesn't work. That's why buying lingerie doesn't work. That's just putting a plaster on it.

3 THINGS TO TRY TONIGHT

1. Name the cycle together. Out loud. "Oh, we're doing the thing again." The cycle becomes the enemy, not your partner.

2. Get underneath. The reacher shares the fear, not the complaint. "I miss you. I'm scared we're losing us." The withdrawer shares what's going on inside instead of disappearing. "I freeze because I feel like I'm failing you."

3. Take the pressure off sex. Completely. Touch that doesn't have to go anywhere. Pleasure that doesn't need a finish line. Orgasm is not the goal. Connection is.

When the avoiding partner feels safe, their desire often comes back, because they feel met.

This is exactly the work I do with couples. EFT for the emotional cycle. Sex therapy and EFT for the sexual cycle. Together, because they're the same problem.
