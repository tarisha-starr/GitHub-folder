# Blog infographics

Four graphics, two per post. Each one is already drawn in the reading version of
the posts as a diagram, so these prompts are for the portrait social versions:
Instagram, Pinterest, and the in post images on WordPress.

Same pipeline as `content/chatgpt_infographic_prompts.md`. Paste one prompt into
a model that generates images, then save the result as
`images/blog/{slug}-{n}.jpg`. The existing `reapply-logo` workflow stamps the
logo on the top right, which is why every prompt reserves that corner.

## Brand

These match the existing 30 exactly, so the blog graphics and the social set
read as one library.

**Palette, locked.** Background Cream `#F5EFE3`. Headlines Near-Black
`#15110D`. Small caps and labels Rust `#9E4A2A`. Accent marks and dividers
Copper `#C75D3D`. Footer bar Navy `#1F2A44` with Cream `#F5EFE3` text. Copper
stays under ten percent of the visible surface.

**Fonts.** Marcellus for headlines, Lora for body and italics. Nothing else.

## 1. The three stages of relationship

*Post: Why You Can Be Best Friends And Still Not Want Each Other*

````
Render exactly the text below in the layout described. Treat every quoted string
as VERBATIM copy. Do not paraphrase, do not add words. Use straight apostrophes.

TITLE (top, large Marcellus serif, Near-Black #15110D, centred, may wrap to
two lines):
"The Three Stages of Relationship"

EYEBROW above the title, Lora small caps, Rust #9E4A2A:
"AFTER DAVID DEIDA, INTIMATE COMMUNION, 1995"

BODY: three stacked rows, equal height, separated by hairline Copper
#C75D3D rules. Each row has a large Marcellus numeral on the left in Copper,
a Marcellus heading, one line of Lora body text under it, and on the right two
small label and dot pairs.

ROW 1. Numeral "1". Heading "Roles by duty".
Body: "He does the money, she does the feelings. Secure, predictable, often
lonely."
Right side, two stacked labels in Lora small caps Rust with a small circle
beside each: "FREEDOM" with a hollow circle outlined in Copper, "CHARGE"
with a circle filled solid Copper.

ROW 2. Numeral "2". Heading "Fifty fifty".
Body: "Equal, independent, negotiated. Nothing is wrong, and nothing is alive."
Right side: "FREEDOM" with a solid filled Copper circle, "CHARGE" with a
hollow circle outlined in Copper.

ROW 3. Numeral "3". Heading "Chosen difference".
Body: "Both free, both choosing this, bringing the difference back on purpose."
Right side: "FREEDOM" solid filled circle, "CHARGE" solid filled circle.

BOTTOM LINE, centred, Lora italic, Near-Black #15110D at 80 percent:
"Most of us traded the charge for the freedom and called it progress."

STYLE
- Canvas 4:5 portrait, 1024x1536 px. Flat editorial design, NOT photorealistic,
  NOT a photograph, no 3D, no gradients, no drop shadows.
- Solid background Cream #F5EFE3. Optional very subtle paper grain.
- Generous whitespace. Calm, premium, editorial. The numerals and the dots are
  the only graphic elements.
- The circles are plain geometric circles, about 20 px, not icons or emoji.

LOGO ZONE: leave the top right corner empty, about 130x130 px. No logo,
monogram, watermark or mark of any kind there.

FOOTER, last 6 percent of canvas: solid Navy #1F2A44 bar, full width,
centred text "SEXUALEMPOWERMENTFORWOMEN.COM" in Marcellus all caps, Cream
#F5EFE3. No other text in the footer.
````

## 3. Desire doesn't come first

*Post: Why You Never Want Sex Until You're Already Having It*

This is the key one. It's the whole post in one image and it's the most likely to
get saved and shared.

````
Render exactly the text below in the layout described. Treat every quoted string
as VERBATIM copy. Do not paraphrase, do not add words. Use straight apostrophes.

TITLE (top, Marcellus, Near-Black #15110D, centred, may wrap to two lines):
"Desire doesn't come first"

EYEBROW above the title, Lora small caps, Rust #9E4A2A:
"AFTER ROSEMARY BASSON, 2000"

UPPER THIRD, labelled in Lora small caps Rust: "THE STRAIGHT LINE, 1966 TO 1979"
Draw four small rectangular outlined chips in a single horizontal row, connected
by thin Copper #C75D3D arrows pointing right. Chip text in Lora, Near-Black
#15110D at 60 percent: "Desire", "Arousal", "Orgasm", "Resolution".

LOWER TWO THIRDS, labelled in Lora small caps Rust: "THE CIRCLE, 2000"
Draw one large open circle, a thin Copper #C75D3D ring, with six evenly
spaced numbered dots around it and three small arrowheads on the ring showing
clockwise direction. Five dots are hollow circles outlined in Copper with
the numeral inside in Near-Black #15110D. The fifth dot, at the lower left of the
ring, is filled solid Copper with its numeral in Cream #F5EFE3.

Label each dot with short Lora text placed outside the ring, clear of it:
1 at the top: "Neutral"
2 upper right: "Willing"
3 lower right: "Stimulus, and a context that works"
4 bottom: "Arousal"
5 lower left, this is the highlighted one: "DESIRE ARRIVES HERE" in Lora small
caps, Rust #9E4A2A
6 upper left: "Satisfaction, then closeness"

BOTTOM LINE, centred, Lora italic, Near-Black #15110D at 80 percent:
"On the line, no desire at the start means a fault. On the circle, it's just
Tuesday."

STYLE
- Canvas 4:5 portrait, 1024x1536 px. Flat editorial diagram, NOT photorealistic,
  no 3D, no gradients, no glow.
- Solid background Cream #F5EFE3.
- This is a clean diagram, so precision matters: the circle is a true circle,
  the six dots are evenly spaced, every label sits fully outside the ring and
  does not overlap it or any other label, and no text is cut off by the canvas
  edge. Leave room around the ring for the labels.
- No icons, no emoji, no illustrations of people or bodies.

LOGO ZONE: leave the top right corner empty, about 130x130 px.

FOOTER, last 6 percent: solid Navy #1F2A44 bar, centred
"SEXUALEMPOWERMENTFORWOMEN.COM" in Marcellus all caps, Cream #F5EFE3.
````
