# Two Weeks of Lunchboxes + Shopping Lists

Built from the structure of the "Lunchbox Kids" system (lunchboxsystem.online).

## What that page actually is

It's a sales page for a $27 recipe ebook, not research. It shows no recipes, no nutrition formula and cites no studies. What it does give is a sensible structure, and that's what this plan borrows:

- **Four food groups** per week: Protein Bites, Healthy Snacks, Healthy Baking, Guilt-Free Sweets
- **One 90-minute batch cook on Sunday**, then Monday to Friday is assembly only
- **Common supermarket ingredients**, nothing fancy
- **Prep the night before** so mornings are just "grab and pack"

Everything below is sized for **two to three lunchboxes a day** and written for hungry teenagers. If yours eat like locusts, double the protein and add a second piece of fruit.

Every box follows the same quiet formula: **one protein, one veg, one fruit, one baked thing or sweet bite, something crunchy.**

## Week 1

### Sunday batch cook (about 90 minutes)

Put the oven on at 200°C first. Then work in this order so things overlap.

1. **Oven chicken nuggets** (Protein Bites)
   Cut 1kg boneless chicken thigh into bite-size pieces. Set up three bowls: flour with salt and paprika, two beaten eggs, panko mixed with a handful of grated parmesan. Coat, lay on a lined tray, spray or drizzle with oil, bake 20 to 25 minutes, flipping once. Cool, then split: half in the fridge, half in the freezer for next week.

2. **Banana oat muffins** (Healthy Baking), makes 12
   Mash 3 very ripe bananas. Mix in 2 eggs, ½ cup Greek yoghurt, ⅓ cup honey, 1 tsp vanilla. Fold in 1 cup wholemeal flour, 1 cup rolled oats, 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda, 1 tsp cinnamon. Optional handful of dark choc chips. Bake 18 to 20 minutes at 180°C. Freeze half.

3. **Energy truffles** (Guilt-Free Sweets), makes about 20
   Blitz 1½ cups pitted dates, 1 cup oats, ⅓ cup peanut butter, 2 tbsp cocoa, pinch of salt. Roll into balls, roll in desiccated coconut. Fridge in a container.

4. **Hummus**
   Blend 1 tin drained chickpeas, 2 tbsp tahini, juice of 1 lemon, 1 garlic clove, 2 tbsp olive oil, salt, a splash of water. Takes four minutes. Or buy a tub and skip this.

5. **Veggie cups**
   Cut carrots, cucumber and capsicum into sticks. Store in a container with a damp paper towel, or in water for the carrots. Rinse cherry tomatoes. Wash grapes.

6. **Boil 6 eggs**, 8 minutes, into cold water, peel on the day.

7. **Cook 250g pasta** for Friday's pasta salad. Toss with a little oil and fridge it.

## Week 2

### Sunday batch cook (about 90 minutes)

Oven at 200°C again.

1. **Hidden-veg meatball bites** (Protein Bites), makes about 30
   Mix 1kg beef mince, 1 grated zucchini (squeeze the water out), 1 grated carrot, 1 egg, ½ cup breadcrumbs, 2 tbsp grated parmesan, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp dried oregano, salt and pepper. Roll small, bake on a lined tray 18 to 20 minutes. Freeze half.

2. **Cheese & spinach scones** (Healthy Baking), makes 12
   Rub 60g cold butter into 2 cups flour (half wholemeal) with 3 tsp baking powder and ½ tsp salt. Stir in 1 cup grated tasty cheese and a big handful of chopped baby spinach. Add ¾ cup milk, bring together gently, pat out 3cm thick, cut squares. Bake 12 to 15 minutes at 220°C. Freeze half.

3. **Zucchini & corn fritters** (Healthy Snacks), makes about 16
   Grate 2 zucchini, salt, squeeze dry. Mix with 1 tin drained corn, 2 eggs, ½ cup flour, ½ cup grated cheese, 2 chopped spring onions, salt and pepper. Pan-fry spoonfuls in a little oil, 3 minutes a side. These are lovely cold with a yoghurt dip.

4. **Carrot loaf** (Guilt-Free Sweets), 1 loaf
   Whisk 2 eggs, ½ cup oil, ½ cup honey or brown sugar, 1 tsp vanilla. Stir in 2 cups grated carrot. Fold in 1½ cups flour, 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda, 2 tsp cinnamon, pinch of salt, optional ½ cup sultanas or chopped walnuts. Loaf tin, 45 to 50 minutes at 170°C.

5. **Mini pancakes**, makes about 24
   1½ cups flour, 1 tbsp sugar, 2 tsp baking powder, pinch salt, 1 egg, 1¼ cups milk, 2 tbsp melted butter. Spoon small rounds into a pan. Cool, stack with baking paper between, freeze in a bag. They double as a quick breakfast.

6. **Veggie cups** again: carrots, cucumber, capsicum, cherry tomatoes. **Boil 6 eggs.**

7. **Yoghurt dip:** 1 cup Greek yoghurt, squeeze of lemon, pinch of salt, chopped mint or a little garlic.

## Little things that make it stick

- **Pack straight from the batch containers.** Line them up on one fridge shelf: protein, baking, dips, cut veg. The page sells this as a "fridge station" upsell. It's just a shelf.
- **Let the teens pack their own.** Give them the formula (protein, veg, fruit, baked, crunch) and step back. They eat more of what they chose.
- **Ice pack every day.** Chicken, meatballs, eggs and yoghurt dip need it.
- **Freezer is your friend.** Half of everything baked goes in the freezer on Sunday. Week 2's nuggets and Week 1's muffins are already done.
- **Swap freely.** Hate tuna? Shredded roast chicken. Nut-free school? Sunflower seed butter in the truffles and on the pancakes.
