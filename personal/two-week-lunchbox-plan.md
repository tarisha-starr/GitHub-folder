# Two Weeks of Lunchboxes + Shopping Lists

Built from what the "Lunchbox Kids" system (lunchboxsystem.online) actually pictures on its page. No purchase needed: every item they show is a standard recipe, written out below.

## What the page shows

Ten recipes are pictured. This plan uses all ten, five per week:

| Pictured item | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| Chicken nuggets | Crumbed chicken pieces, served with tomato sauce |
| Ham & cheese sticks | Crumbed bread rolls with ham and melty cheese inside |
| Meatball bites | Golden crumbed balls with a cheese centre, more croquette than meatball |
| Veggie cups | Little pastry cups filled with cheese and spinach |
| Cheese scones | Classic cheese scones |
| Banana muffins | Banana muffins with a slice on top |
| Mini pancakes | Silver-dollar pancakes, eaten cold with fruit |
| Carrot loaf | Carrot loaf with a drizzle of icing |
| Healthy cookies | Chunky oat cookies, Anzac style |
| Energy truffles | Date and oat balls rolled in coconut or cocoa |

Their lunchbox photo is: a main, cheese cubes, carrot sticks, grapes, crackers. That's the whole formula. The bonus menu promises "one main plus one salad per day" and a 90-minute Sunday batch cook, so that's how this is laid out.

Sized for **two to three lunchboxes a day**, written for teenagers. Double the main if yours eat like locusts.

**Every box:** one main, something crunchy, one veg, one fruit, one sweet or baked bite.

## Week 1

### Sunday batch cook (about 90 minutes)

Oven on at 200°C. Work in this order so trays overlap.

1. **Chicken nuggets** (main)
   Cut 1kg boneless chicken thigh into bite-size pieces. Three bowls: flour with salt and paprika, 2 beaten eggs, panko with a handful of grated parmesan. Coat, lay on a lined tray, drizzle or spray with oil, bake 20 to 25 minutes, flipping once. Cool. Half in the fridge, half in the freezer.

2. **Ham & cheese sticks** (main), makes 12
   Cut the crusts off 12 slices of sandwich bread and roll each one flat with a rolling pin. Lay a slice of ham and a stick of cheese (cut a block into fingers, or use string cheese) on each, roll up tight. Dip in beaten egg, roll in breadcrumbs, place seam-down on a lined tray. Spray with oil, bake 12 to 15 minutes at 200°C until golden. These are great cold. Freeze half before baking if you like.

3. **Banana muffins** (baked), makes 12
   Mash 3 very ripe bananas. Mix in 2 eggs, ½ cup Greek yoghurt, ⅓ cup honey, 1 tsp vanilla. Fold in 1 cup wholemeal flour, 1 cup plain flour, 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda, 1 tsp cinnamon. Top each with a slice of banana. Bake 18 to 20 minutes at 180°C. Freeze half.

4. **Oat cookies** (sweet), makes about 20
   Melt 125g butter with 2 tbsp golden syrup. Mix 1 cup oats, 1 cup flour, ¾ cup desiccated coconut, ½ cup brown sugar. Dissolve 1 tsp baking soda in 2 tbsp boiling water, add to the butter, pour over the dry mix. Roll balls, flatten, bake 12 minutes at 170°C.

5. **Energy truffles** (sweet), makes about 20
   Blitz 1½ cups pitted dates, 1 cup oats, ⅓ cup peanut butter, 2 tbsp cocoa, pinch of salt. Roll into balls, roll in coconut. Fridge. Nut-free school? Swap peanut butter for sunflower seed butter.

6. **Sides prep:** carrot and cucumber sticks in a container with a damp paper towel, cherry tomatoes rinsed, grapes washed, cheese block cut into cubes, 6 eggs boiled.

## Week 2

### Sunday batch cook (about 90 minutes)

Oven on at 200°C.

1. **Cheesy meatball bites** (main), makes about 30
   Mix 1kg beef mince, 1 egg, ½ cup breadcrumbs, 2 tbsp grated parmesan, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp dried oregano, salt and pepper. Take a walnut-size lump, push a cube of mozzarella or tasty cheese into the middle, seal it in. Roll in beaten egg then breadcrumbs. Bake on a lined tray 18 to 20 minutes. Freeze half. (Secret option: grate a carrot into the mince. It disappears.)

2. **Ham & cheese pastry cups** (main), makes 12
   The page's version is spinach and cheese. For kids who won't touch green, this is the same thing with ham. Cut 2 sheets of thawed puff pastry into 12 squares. Press each square into a greased muffin tin so the corners stick up. Whisk 4 eggs with ¼ cup milk, salt and pepper. Into each cup: a little chopped ham, a pinch of grated cheese, then pour egg to three-quarters full. Bake 18 to 20 minutes until puffed and golden. Add a tablespoon of finely chopped spinach to the egg if yours will tolerate it.

3. **Cheese scones** (baked), makes 12
   Rub 60g cold butter into 2 cups flour with 3 tsp baking powder and ½ tsp salt. Stir in 1½ cups grated tasty cheese. Add ¾ cup milk, bring together gently, pat out 3cm thick, cut rounds or squares. Brush tops with milk. Bake 12 to 15 minutes at 220°C. Freeze half.

4. **Carrot loaf** (sweet), 1 loaf
   Whisk 2 eggs, ½ cup oil, ½ cup brown sugar, 1 tsp vanilla. Stir in 2 cups grated carrot. Fold in 1½ cups flour, 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda, 2 tsp cinnamon, pinch of salt. Loaf tin, 45 to 50 minutes at 170°C. Icing: ½ cup icing sugar with a squeeze of lemon, drizzled over once cool. Optional chopped walnuts on top (skip for nut-free).

5. **Mini pancakes**, makes about 24
   1½ cups flour, 1 tbsp sugar, 2 tsp baking powder, pinch salt, 1 egg, 1¼ cups milk, 2 tbsp melted butter. Spoon small rounds into a pan, flip when bubbles pop. Cool, stack with baking paper between, freeze in a bag. Pack frozen; they thaw by lunch. Doubles as breakfast.

6. **Sides prep:** carrot and cucumber sticks, cherry tomatoes, grapes, cheese cubes, 6 boiled eggs.

## Little things that make it stick

- **One fridge shelf, lined up.** Mains, baking, cut veg, dips. The page sells this as a "fridge station" upsell. It's a shelf.
- **Let the teens pack their own.** Give them the formula (main, crunch, veg, fruit, sweet) and step back. They eat more of what they chose.
- **Ice pack every day.** Chicken, meatball bites, pastry cups and eggs need it.
- **Freezer is your friend.** Half of everything goes in the freezer on Sunday. Week 2's nuggets and Week 1's muffins are already done by the time you need them.
- **Swap freely.** Picky about one thing? Every main here is "something wrapped in crumbs or pastry", so swap the filling, keep the method.
