Before you apply

The questions people ask me first.

My partner will not come. Is there any point?
Yes, and it is more common than you would think. Plenty of people start on their own because one of them is not ready. Coming by yourself still shifts the pattern, because a pattern needs both people to keep running it. Say so on the application and we will talk about it.
We are in Australia, the UK, anywhere else. Can we still work with you?
Yes. We meet on Zoom, so it does not matter where in the world you are. You do not have to leave the house. Some couples I see have never been to Auckland. We will find a time that works across the time difference.
What does the free session actually involve?
A conversation, not an assessment. We look at what is happening between you now, and we map the cycle you are caught in. You leave with a clearer view of what is driving it and a next step, whether or not you decide to work with me. Nothing to pay and no obligation.
There has been an affair. Is it too late?
Not necessarily. Attachment injuries, including infidelity, are precisely what Emotionally Focused Therapy was built to work with. Repair is slow and it is not guaranteed, but it is possible far more often than people believe when they are in the worst of it.
How quickly will I hear back?
Within one to three business days. I read every application myself, so it is not instant, but it is not a queue either. If it is not a fit I will tell you honestly and suggest where to look instead.
