# Wild Sky Coffee — Product Pages

A template, then paste-ready copy for all ten products, then the FAQ page.

Your existing descriptions are better than most gear copy on the internet. I have
kept the lines that work and added the three things every one of them is missing:
**a one-line "pick this if"**, **what it fits with**, and **a reason to trust the
purchase at the point of the button**.

---

## The product page template

Order matters. Nic decides on the specs, Sarah decides on the photo and the
story, and both of them need the trust line before they'll press the button.

1. **Photos** — in use outdoors, plus one on a plain background with something
   for scale, plus one nested inside whatever it nests inside
2. **Title** — product name
3. **Price** + a spec strip: `225ml · 85g · double-wall titanium`
4. **"Pick this if…"** — one sentence
5. **Add to cart**
6. **Trust bar** — free NZ shipping · dispatched from Auckland in 1–3 days ·
   30-day returns · in stock
7. **Description** — your existing voice, two or three short paragraphs
8. **Specs table** — including dimensions, which every page is currently missing
9. **What it fits with** — nests inside / works with (measured, not assumed)
10. **Goes well with** — two or three cross-sells
11. **Reviews**
12. **Short FAQ** — two or three questions specific to this product

---

## Fix first: three spec problems

1. **Wild Coffee Scooper contradicts itself** — the description says "at just
   25g", the spec table says 26g. Pick one.
2. **Alpine Pot at 1000ml / 79g** is extraordinarily light for a one-litre
   titanium pot. If that number is right it deserves to be the headline of the
   page. If it's a typo, the people you most want as customers will be the ones
   who spot it. Weigh it before this page goes live.
3. **No product has dimensions published** except the Brew Table. Height and
   diameter decide whether something fits a pack pocket or nests in a pot, and
   trampers will not guess. Measure all ten.
