## Hero

**Headline — three options**

1. *Benefit-led:* **Titanium coffee gear light enough to earn its place in your pack**
2. *Outcome-led:* **A proper coffee, three days from the nearest café**
3. *Curiosity-driven:* **The heaviest thing in your pack shouldn't be the coffee kit**

My pick is 1. It is the only one that says what you sell, who it's for and why
it's different in one line, and "earns its place" is already your own phrase from
Our Story.

**Subheadline**

> Designed in the New Zealand backcountry. From a 45g mug to a press that makes
> coffee for two — built to be carried, not admired in a cupboard.

**Buttons**

- Primary: **Shop the coffee gear**
- Secondary: **Which cup should I get?** → links to the comparison block below

"Show me" is friendly but it doesn't tell anyone where they're going. And the
second button is the question most of your visitors are actually holding.

**Under the buttons, small type**

> Free NZ shipping · Dispatched from Auckland in 1–3 days · 30-day returns

## One note on the thing you're tempted to add

No countdown timers, no "only 2 left" unless it's true, no invented scarcity.
You have a genuine story, genuine free shipping and genuinely tested gear.
Manufactured urgency would make all three look less credible — and a discount
claim that isn't true to its own terms is exactly what the Fair Trading Act 1986
is there to stop.
