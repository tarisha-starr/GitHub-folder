# Conversion treatment by page

The full conversion kit belongs on pages where money changes hands. Putting a
sticky booking bar and an urgency band on a blog post or a privacy policy makes
the whole site feel like a funnel, which is the opposite of what a therapy
practice needs to communicate.

## Tier 1, the full treatment

Hero image with gradient fallback, sticky booking bar, offer band above the
reviews, risk-reversal guarantee, FAQ answering the top objections, one-time
dismissible nudge after pricing, count-up stats, draw-on checkmarks in the
pricing list, breathe on the featured tier.

- `/together/retreats/wanaka` — flagship. Its ten sections are already specced
  in Notion under `4. Sales Page, The Wanaka Adventure`. Follow that copy.
- `/together/retreats/queenstown` — duplicate of Wanaka once approved
- `/together/intensives` — The New Chapter of Us, NZ$15,000
- `/for-her/course` — SERW, the three month programme
- `/for-her/membership` — Radiant Woman Circle, $97/month

## Tier 2, partial

Hero image, sticky bar, guarantee, FAQ, hover lifts, scroll progress. No
discount band, no countdown, no exit nudge: these pages are choosing a
direction, not closing a sale.

- `/together/therapy` — the services page
- `/together` and `/for-her` landings
- `/together/retreats` hub, `/for-her/retreats`

## Tier 3, light

Motion and hero image only. These pages build trust; pressure damages them.

- `/`, `/about`
- `/for-her/quiz` and `/for-her/mini-course` — free opt-ins, so the nudge is
  an email capture rather than a discount

## No conversion treatment

`/podcast`, `/blog`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`.

## Honest urgency

The brand has genuine scarcity and does not need invented scarcity. Six couples
per retreat, fixed dates, a named month. "Six places, two remaining for March"
outperforms "only 3 slots left this week" because it is specific, and it is
defensible if anyone asks.

A hardcoded countdown that resets on every page load is a misleading
representation under the Fair Trading Act 1986. If urgency is used, it has to
be driven by an actual number the user maintains, in a WordPress field or a
constant that someone updates when a place sells.

Instead of a small discount on a premium service, reach for: early-booking
terms, a deposit that holds a place, a payment plan, or the free twenty minute
call. These raise conversion without signalling that the price was soft.
